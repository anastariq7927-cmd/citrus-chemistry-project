# Chemistry of Citrus Fruits: Applications in Green Chemistry

This project explores the chemical properties of compounds extracted from citrus fruits and their potential applications in sustainable industrial processes.

The study investigates how naturally occurring compounds can contribute to greener alternatives in cosmetics, water treatment, and the food industry.

## Topics Covered

- **Buffering Capacity:** Theoretical and experimental comparison of citric acid and acetic acid based on pH measurements.
- **Metal Chelation:** Study of citrate ion complexation with copper(II) ions and its potential applications.
- **Naringin Extraction:** Investigation of the extraction of a natural antioxidant from citrus fruits.

## Objectives

The aim is to understand how compounds derived from citrus fruits can help reduce the environmental impact of industrial processes through biodegradable and low-toxicity alternatives.
